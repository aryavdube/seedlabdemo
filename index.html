(02)  The central question

Why do some young people with shared familial liability progress to serious mental illness — while others remain resilient?

We look for the earliest developmental paraphenotypes.

Paraphenotypes are traits that emerge in parallel with clinical symptoms, arising from shared neurodevelopmental systems. Identifying them early could reveal which children are on which path — years before illness takes hold.

By translating these multidimensional signatures into scalable, clinically actionable tools, we aim to enable earlier identification of risk, more personalised intervention, and developmentally informed, preemptive psychiatry.
